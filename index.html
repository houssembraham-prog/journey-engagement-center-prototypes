Level of autonomy

Engagements

4 of 4 active

Engagements are abstract patterns ACE can act on. The patterns are open-ended; these are the ones that recur often enough to be worth naming and bounding. What each means for a given shopper is ACE's call. Activated means ACE is permitted to act on it; deactivated means it won't. This list is not definitive, and will change with time.

Flows

The standard lifecycle sequences, running on triggers you can name.

A flow fires on a trigger you can name, every time it happens. That is the whole difference from Engagements, where ACE works the moments no fixed trigger describes. Browse Abandoned catches the session that ended; ACE reads the one that is still going. We write the trigger — it is what makes a Welcome a Welcome, and it is not yours to move. The wait is yours, and so is what the message says.
FlowWhen — the trigger Sent · 30dCVRStatus

Every flow ships on. Pausing one stops it entirely — the shoppers who would have entered it simply don't. The guardrails still run last on anything a flow sends: quiet hours, frequency caps and the exclusion list all apply here exactly as they do to ACE.
